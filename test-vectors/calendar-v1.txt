# Calendar v1 normative vectors.
# Timestamps are unsigned decimal milliseconds since 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z.
# Month indices count calendar months from January 1970, which is index 0.
#
# Every value here is derived twice: once by tools/calendar-vectors/expected.py,
# which imports nothing from simulation/ and walks month lengths from 1970, and
# once by a live run of simulation/calendar, which uses the closed-form era
# arithmetic. A boolean vector may only be true, because its name is the claim.
#
# The scenario is simulation/calendar/scenario.py. Its timestamps sit at the
# last and first millisecond of a month, at a repeated value, and on both sides
# of a halt long enough to leave two months without a single height, because an
# off-by-one is invisible at an instant in the middle of a month.
