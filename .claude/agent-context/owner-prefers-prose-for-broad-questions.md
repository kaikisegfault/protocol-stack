# Owner prefers prose for broad questions

On 2026-08-19 the owner cancelled a four-question `AskUserQuestion` batch about an
AI-decentralization pivot and answered every point in one raw prose message
instead, saying it let them "cover everything more flexible".

**Why:** the questions were architectural and interlocking. Four fixed options
each could not express answers like "zero-winner and dust go to one pool that
pays the next cycle's best performers" — an answer that invents a mechanism
rather than picking one. Constraining that to options loses information.

**How to apply:** keep `CLAUDE.md`'s rule — narrow founder-reserved *value*
choices (a threshold, a beneficiary, a rejection order) still go through the
selectable-option tool, batched and last. But when the open items are
architectural, interlocking, or likely to produce a mechanism the options do not
contain, state the analysis and the specific gaps in prose and invite a free-form
answer. Say plainly what is unresolved and why it blocks, rather than forcing a
menu. See `owner-invites-surface-logic-questions.md`.
